What happens when one flow stops?

The drill supply chain is operating normally. Components, information, and money move between participants—until one flow suddenly fails.

Physical flow

Components and completed drills move through facilities and transport links. Without it, production and delivery cannot continue.

Information flow

Orders, schedules, stock records, shipping instructions, and confirmations coordinate action. Without it, participants do not know what to do.

Financial flow

Customer payments, invoices, freight charges, and supplier payments sustain participation. Without it, future supply and cooperation are threatened.

    Key takeaway

    The three flows are interdependent.

    Different starting points. The same network-wide consequence.

    Logistics moves the drill—but uninterrupted information, physical, and financial flows make that movement possible.

    What happens when each flow stops?

    • Information stops first

      Participants lack the instructions or data to know what to order, produce, release, or deliver. Physical movement and payments are delayed.

    • Physical flow stops first

      The required component or product does not arrive. Production, inventory availability, and delivery stop.

    • Financial flow stops first

      A participant is not paid. Future supply, production capacity, and cooperation may soon stop.
